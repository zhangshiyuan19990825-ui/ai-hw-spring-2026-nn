Weights Summary

The trained model weights are saved in the results/weights folder.

Files:
- mlp.pt: trained weights for the MLP model
- cnn.pt: trained weights for the CNN model
- transformer.pt: trained weights for the Transformer Encoder model

In this project, the weights were not manually assigned. They were initialized by PyTorch and updated during training through backpropagation and the Adam optimizer.

The model with the best result was CNN, with 98.80% test accuracy on the MNIST test set.
